# Regional website pricing research

Historical research: the later international location-page task supersedes these calculated prices with explicit approved amounts. See [current pricing](pricing.md) and the [location-copy report](international-location-copy.md). The research below is retained as context, not the current pricing policy.

Research date: **21 October 2026**. This supersedes the previous UGX currency-conversion approach. The user requested local package estimates for the US, UK, Canada and Australia, discounted by **15%**, and authorized using the resulting prices. Uganda and the homepage retain UGX 900,000 / 1,800,000 / 3,500,000.

## Method and limits

Compare one-off, professionally delivered website builds in each country's own currency. Match features rather than package names: VeilCode Starter includes up to 20 pages; Business adds CMS, bookings, lead handling and support; Ecommerce adds checkout, shipping, inventory, promotions and order management. Five-page introductory packages are context, not equivalent Starter quotes.

The benchmarks below are **our scope-adjusted planning estimates**, informed by the published provider prices below. They are not statistical national averages, live quotations for VeilCode's exact feature lists, or medians of the cited packages. In particular, competitor page/product limits, support periods and included hosting differ. Unlimited CMS pages or products do not mean an unlimited initial content-entry or custom-development workload; the written scope still determines delivery.

Use the public build prices as anchors, then select a rounded benchmark for a comparable, modest initial deployment of each VeilCode tier. The US Starter estimate tracks a published ten-page custom build near USD 3,900 while allowing for a larger standard site; more capable tiers use integration/store benchmarks. UK estimates sit above budget micro-sites and allow for wider scope and support. Canadian estimates bridge lower-cost independent work and agency packages. Australian estimates use twenty-page, CMS and custom-commerce prices rather than five-page Launch offers.

Provider taxes, platform subscriptions and ongoing fees are not uniform. They are identified where known; no claim is made that the sources are fully tax-normalized. We have not silently added another country's tax to VeilCode's prices. Final scope, price and billing currency are confirmed in the proposal. This research establishes build-price positioning, not a margin/cost-of-delivery analysis or a promise to be cheaper than every competing provider.

## Primary-source observations

### United States

- [Sitefyd US packages](https://us.sitefyd.com/affordable-website-design-packages): USD 3,900 for ten custom pages with a blog and SEO; custom/integration work from USD 8,000. Its five-page USD 1,490 tier is smaller than VeilCode Starter. These anchor the larger standard site and integration-heavy Business estimate.
- [Sitefyd US ecommerce guide](https://us.sitefyd.com/ecommerce-website-cost/): describes growth stores of 25–250 products at USD 6,500–14,000. A USD 10,000 Ecommerce benchmark sits inside that provider's stated planning range; it is our selection, not a quoted exact VeilCode equivalent.
- [Websloop pricing](https://websloop.com/pricing): the inspected live page lists ten-page custom sites from USD 1,499 and ecommerce/booking systems from USD 2,499; older search snippets showed higher numbers. This is a lower-priced counterpoint: our estimates are not the market floor, and individual competitors may still quote below VeilCode's final prices.

### United Kingdom

- [Sketchanet packages](https://www.sketchanet.design/website-design-packages): around twenty pages from GBP 1,200, with more involved premium work from GBP 2,500. Ecommerce is separately scoped. This supports the Starter estimate of GBP 1,500 for a larger standard website.
- [Codarji prices](https://www.codarji.com/pricing/): twelve-page CMS/business sites at GBP 2,450 and online stores at GBP 3,750, with payments, shipping and order handling. Our GBP 3,000 Business and GBP 4,000 Ecommerce estimates allow for VeilCode's wider feature/support scope; they are inferred, not Codarji quotes.
- [North & Form pricing](https://www.northandform.co.uk/pricing): booking sites from GBP 2,300 and ecommerce from GBP 2,750; the page says its figures include VAT. Hosting and care are separate. This cross-checks the functionality tiers without being treated as a tax-matched quote.
- [EverLabs pricing](https://www.everlabsdesign.com/pricing/): a budget alternative advertises twenty pages with booking at GBP 900 and a shop at GBP 1,100; hosting is additional. These lower quotes were considered rather than represented as the whole UK market.

### Canada

- [WynneWebDesign packages](https://wynnewebdesign.com/services/web-design/): explicitly CAD; five pages from 2,500, ten-page Business from 5,500, and twenty pages with ecommerce from 9,000. Extra pages are CAD 200 each; blog setup is extra. Its small Starter is not the same size as VeilCode's.
- [SprintMarkt's own published prices](https://sprintmarkt.ca/en/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost-in-canada-2026/): business builds from CAD 5,900 and ecommerce from CAD 8,900. Those are provider starting prices, not a national survey. They support a CAD 6,500 enhanced Business estimate and a CAD 9,000 store estimate.
- [Vanguard Media website pricing](https://vanguardmedia.ca/services/web-design): complete Business websites from CAD 3,500, while custom websites are quoted to scope. This supplies another lower-priced business-site anchor; its separate software/MVP prices were not treated as website package equivalents.
- **Starter reasoning:** a CAD 4,000 planning estimate falls between smaller independent-site pricing and the larger agency-business tier. VeilCode's twenty standard pages are accommodated through agreed layouts/content; a bespoke design and written copy for every page could cost more. This is deliberately a scope judgment, not an arithmetic average.

### Australia

- [DIGITALON website pricing](https://digitalon.com.au/website-design-pricing): AUD 4,990 for twenty custom pages, SEO, blog capability and analytics. This is the closest published page-count anchor for the AUD 5,000 Starter estimate. Its ecommerce pricing information explicitly describes Australian-dollar prices with GST included.
- [Kavera pricing](https://www.kavera.com.au/pricing): Business AUD 4,000–9,000; unlimited-page Premium CMS AUD 9,000–14,000; custom ecommerce AUD 7,000–13,000. Its figures are plus GST. The AUD 8,000 Business estimate sits between standard business and premium CMS scope; the AUD 10,000 store estimate is within custom-commerce pricing.
- [DIGITALON ecommerce pricing](https://digitalon.com.au/ecommerce-pricing): twenty-page stores at AUD 7,990, with more advanced shopping features; ongoing services are separate. This is a lower counterpoint to the custom-commerce estimate.
- [PixelLab packages](https://pixellabstudio.com.au/pricing): twelve-page CMS/booking work AUD 3,999, and twenty-page ecommerce/advanced integrations AUD 6,999. Unlimited-page systems require a custom quote. This confirms the breadth of local prices and why the estimates are positioning decisions rather than universal averages.

Search snippets were checked against live pages where used. A cached Digitari snippet quoted fixed CAD tiers, but the live page had changed to monthly and custom-quote offers; those stale fixed numbers were excluded. The older Datum PDF marked its pricing accurate as of April 2024, so it was not used as a current benchmark anchor. Website Mill's page did not clearly establish currency in the inspected pricing text, so its dollar figures were excluded from the numeric CAD anchors.

## Implementation and validation

`REGIONAL_PRICE_BENCHMARKS` and `REGIONAL_DISCOUNT_PERCENT` in `components/pricing/pricing-data.ts` hold the policy. The old FX snapshot, conversions, Approx. label and attribution are removed. The same server-rendered, removable component remains, with no pricing API calls or new client code. Website pricing can still be hidden everywhere with `WEBSITE_PRICING_ENABLED = false`.

Validation results are recorded in [pricing.md](pricing.md).
