# AI Automation Asset Prompts

Use these prompts when generating new VeilCode campaign visuals, proposal sections, or social assets.

## Image Prompt

Create a premium dark technical brand graphic for VeilCode Studio, a digital solutions agency building AI automation systems. Use a deep black and midnight background, neon cyan primary accents, subtle green and violet workflow nodes, thin grid lines, connected automation paths, and clean enterprise SaaS composition. The image should feel precise, senior, operational, and engineering-led. Avoid generic robots, stock photos, cartoon mascots, or overly busy cyberpunk effects.

## Social Post Prompt

Design a square social post for VeilCode Studio about AI automation audits. Headline: "Find the work your team should never do twice." Use a dark technical background, cyan VeilCode accent, small connected workflow nodes, restrained typography, and space for a short footer URL: veilcode.studio.

## Proposal Cover Prompt

Design a 16:9 proposal cover for VeilCode Studio titled "AI automation systems for serious operations." Use a dark grid background, cyan logo mark, connected workflow diagram elements, and a polished B2B software aesthetic. The design should work for a client deck, not a consumer ad.

## Copy Prompt

Write VeilCode Studio copy in a direct, senior, technical voice. Focus on business outcomes, operational clarity, automation ROI, reliable systems, integrations, dashboards, and human review where needed. Avoid vague AI hype, excessive adjectives, and generic productivity claims.

## Workflow Diagram Prompt

Create a clean workflow map with five nodes: Trigger, Agent, Review, Action, Report. Use VeilCode colors: cyan for trigger, green for agent, violet for review, amber for action, and indigo for report. Show directional connections and leave room under the diagram for outcome metrics.
